1. What it is, in one breath

Say: "Everything an insurer does — selling a policy, taking the money, paying a claim, paying suppliers and staff — writes its own accounting automatically. Finance never re-types anything. At month end the books already balance."

Three ideas explain the whole product:

  1. Every business action posts its own journal. Issue a policy → the ledger gets the premium, VAT and stamp duty lines. Pay a claim → the ledger gets it. No manual journals for daily work.
  2. You always see the accounting before it happens. Every button that moves money shows a preview of the debits and credits first. Confirm, and it posts.
  3. Two people for anything important. The person who prepares can never be the person who approves (segregation of duties). Approval limits route big amounts up the chain.

Screens

Vue 3 pages inside Laravel (Inertia). Same components everywhere: lists, record pages, drawers, journal previews.

Application

Laravel 13, PHP 8.4. Code is split into modules: Insurance, Finance, People, Accounting, Platform, Distribution.

Database

PostgreSQL 17, 146 tables. Multi-tenant: every row carries a tenant id and the database itself blocks cross-tenant reads (row-level security).

Background

A queue worker posts accounting events; a scheduler runs nightly jobs (policy activation, reminders, renewals, earning).

Say: "One database, one app, any number of insurance companies as tenants — each one's data is fenced off by the database itself, not just by the app."

3. How money becomes accounting

flowchart LR
  A["Business action
issue policy, pay claim, post payroll…"] --> E["Accounting event
POLICY_ISSUED, CLAIM_PAID…"]
  E --> R["Posting rule
which accounts, which side"]
  R --> J["Journal
balanced debits = credits"]
  J --> L["Ledger → trial balance, P&L, balance sheet"]

57 posting rules cover every event. A rule is data (JSON), not code, so an accountant can change which account an event hits without a developer. Example, a motor policy issued for 14,568.75:

AccountDebitCreditPlain caption
1100 Premium receivable14,568.75Customer owes us the premium
2100 Unearned premium12,625.00Cover not yet provided
2150 VAT payable1,893.75VAT we collect for the government
2155 Stamp duty payable50.00Stamp duty for the government

Each night, premium is earned 1/365 per day (unearned → income). At month end, sub-ledgers (premium, claims, commission, payables, reinsurance, assets, payroll) are reconciled to the general ledger before the period can lock.

employees, payroll runs, payslips"]

accounting_events → journals → journal_lines

  CLM --> ACC
  RI --> ACC
  FIN --> ACC

  • Business tables hold what happened (a policy, a bill). Accounting tables hold what it cost. The link is the accounting event, so every journal line can be traced back to its source document — and every screen lets you click through.
  • Nothing is deleted. Corrections are reversals. Every change is audited (who, when, what).
  • Document numbers are branch-coded: POL-HO-2026-000008, RCT-CTG-2026-000001.

8. How to operate it

Daily rhythm

  1. Open Home. The queues are your to-do list: what is waiting for you, oldest first. Start actions are on the right.
  2. Work from the record. Every next step is offered on the page you are on (after issue → receipt, after reserve → approve).
  3. Confirm the preview. Read the journal lines, then confirm. Nothing posts until you do.
  4. Approvals inbox for anything routed to you, with the lines it will post.

Monthly rhythm

  1. Import bank statements, match, explain exceptions.
  2. Payroll: calculate (HR) → approve (finance) → pay (accountant).
  3. Payables: bills → payment run → bank file.
  4. Month-end close: run the checklist top to bottom; each task posts or reconciles. Pending journals are listed; the lock waits for them.
  5. Soft-lock on the last day, hard-lock after month end. Quarter end: technical provisions and IDRA returns.

Starting the system (developer machine)

docker compose up -d
composer db:fresh && php artisan erp:demo     # fresh demo company
npm run build
php artisan serve --port=8765
php artisan queue:work --queue=posting,batch,recon,default
php artisan schedule:work

Open http://nonlife.localhost:8765. A new company starts at Admin → Setup: company, fiscal year, chart of accounts (from a template), first product with tariff, bank account, limits.

9. Things to say when asked

September cannot be locked on 14 Sep. The soft lock opens on the month's last day; the hard lock after month end. The CFO can lock early with a written reason. Say: a deliberate control.
If they askAnswer
Can we change the accounts an event posts to?Yes. Posting rules are data; account roles map to your chart. No code change.
What about our tax rates / IDRA forms?Every rate and form layout is configuration, currently placeholders marked "verify". We load yours during setup.
Bangla?Switch in the user menu: help, tour, captions, risk labels, payslips. Documents print in English or Bangla.
Multiple branches?Yes. Branch-coded numbers, branch-scoped users see only their branch, budgets and petty cash per branch.
Phone?Home, policy, receipt and claim pages work on a phone.
Not built yetCustomer/agent portals, real SMS and email (logged today), excess-of-loss treaties, co-insurance, leave and attendance, bulk policy import. Next phase.
