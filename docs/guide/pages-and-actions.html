Insuryn Pages and Actions

Every page in the menu: what it shows, each button on it, who may press it, and what happens. Companion to Insuryn Explained From Zero, which explains the ideas.

How to read: "Preview" means the button first shows the journal lines it will post; nothing posts until you confirm. "Roles" names the demo role that holds the permission; the admin can grant it to anyone under Admin → Roles. Roles: officer = branch officer, manager = branch manager, CO/CM = claims officer/manager, acct = accountant, FM = finance manager, CFO, HR = HR manager.
