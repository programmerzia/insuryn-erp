# Fixed assets

## What this screen is for
The register lists the company's furniture, computers, vehicles and fit-outs with their cost, depreciation and net book value. Each asset belongs to a class that sets its depreciation method, useful life and accounts.

## What happens in the accounting
Capitalising an asset posts its cost to the class's cost account, paid from the bank or owed to the supplier. The monthly depreciation batch posts each asset's charge to depreciation expense and accumulated depreciation, and a disposal posts the gain or loss.

## Next step
