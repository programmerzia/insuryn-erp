# Producers and distribution

## What this screen is for
Producers are everyone who brings business: agents, agencies, BDOs, brokers and partners, each with an IDRA licence that must be valid to sell. *Hierarchy* shows who reports to whom from a date, *Compensation schemes* say how each product pays its producers, and *Targets* compare what each producer, branch or channel was set with what they produced.

## What happens in the accounting
Nothing is posted from these screens. Commission is accrued when customers pay, by the scheme in force and the hierarchy on that date, so a move in the tree changes only later commission. An advance paid to a producer is recovered from their commission statements.
