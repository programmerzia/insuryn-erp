# Month-end close

## What this screen is for
The close is the checklist that finishes a month. It earns the month's premium, compares each register with the ledger (premium, claims, commission), checks the bank reconciliation and the age of suspense items, and each task shows a green tick or what to fix.

## What happens in the accounting
Earning moves one twelfth of every annual policy from Unearned premium to Premium income. After the trial balance and statements are signed off the period is locked: nobody can post into it again, and corrections go into the next month as reversals.
