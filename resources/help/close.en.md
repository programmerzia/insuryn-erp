# Month-end close

## What this screen is for
The close is the checklist that finishes a month: it earns the month's premium, compares each register with the ledger (premium, claims, commission, unearned premium, suspense, VAT and stamp duty), checks the bank and the age of suspense items, and each task shows a green tick or what to fix. The nightly jobs that start and expire policies, send payment reminders and earn premium show when they last ran, and finance can run one now.

## What happens in the accounting
Earning moves the premium for the time on cover from Unearned premium reserve to Premium income, and it shows its journal before it runs. In a fiscal year's last month the year-end close moves every income and expense balance to Retained earnings, and the next fiscal year can be opened from this screen. After the trial balance and statements are signed off the period is locked: nobody can post into it again, and corrections go into the next month as reversals.
