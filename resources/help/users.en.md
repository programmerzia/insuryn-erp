# Users and roles

## What this screen is for
Invite the people who use the system and give each one roles, for the whole company or for one branch. A role is a set of permissions; the shipped roles follow the usual jobs of a non-life insurer and can be edited.

## What happens in the accounting
Nothing is posted here, but roles decide who may move money. Some duties must stay with different people, such as requesting and paying a refund, or entering and approving a journal, and the screen warns when a role would combine them. Every change of roles is kept in the audit trail.
