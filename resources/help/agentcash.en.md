# Agent cash

## What this screen is for
Agents often collect premium in cash from customers. This screen shows, for each agent, the cash collected, what has been deposited to the bank, what is still held and for how many days.

## What happens in the accounting
Cash an agent collects reduces what the customer owes and becomes Agent collections receivable, money the agent holds for the company. Recording a deposit moves it from the agent to the bank, and the ledger column must equal what is still held. Cash held for many days is a risk to follow up.
