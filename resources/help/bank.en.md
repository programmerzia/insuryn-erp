# Bank

## What this screen is for
Import your bank statement as a CSV file and check it against the ledger. The system suggests matches by amount, reference and date; accept the ones that are right.

## What happens in the accounting
Matching posts nothing: it proves that what the system recorded from receipts and payments really went through the bank. Lines left over are exceptions to chase, such as bank charges or a transfer nobody has recorded, so explain them or record the missing receipt.

## Next step
When every line up to the month end is matched or explained, the bank task in the month-end close turns green.
