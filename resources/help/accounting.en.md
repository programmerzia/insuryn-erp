# Accounting

## What this screen is for
Journals are the ledger entries the system writes behind every insurance action, so you rarely write one yourself. Use them to check what was recorded, and the trial balance to see the totals by account.

## What happens in the accounting
A posted journal never changes: a mistake is corrected with a reversal on a later date. Manual journals, such as office expenses or accruals, need a second person to approve them before they post. The registers (policies, claims, commission) hold the detail and the ledger holds the totals; the two must agree, and checking that is reconciliation.
