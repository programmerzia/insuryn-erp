# Petty cash

## What this screen is for
Each branch keeps a petty cash float, held by its custodian, for small expenses such as couriers, photocopies and repairs. Every payment is a voucher with the receipt attached, and the cash is counted from time to time.

## What happens in the accounting
Issuing a float moves money from the bank to the petty cash account. A voucher posts the expense against petty cash, a replenishment tops the float up from the bank, and a count posts any shortage or surplus.

## Next step
Request replenishment when the float runs low; the finance manager approves it. Check the petty cash book at month end.
