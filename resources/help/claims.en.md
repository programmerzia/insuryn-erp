# Claims

## What this screen is for
A claim starts when a customer reports a loss: register it against the policy with the date of loss and a description, and nothing financial happens yet. When the surveyor estimates the loss, set the reserve, the amount the company expects to pay.

## What happens in the accounting
Setting the reserve records Claims incurred and an Outstanding claims liability, which is what finance and the regulator watch most. Approving the settlement moves that amount to Claims payable; a different person in finance then releases the payment, which reduces the bank. Closing the claim releases whatever reserve is left.
