# Chart of accounts

## What this screen is for
The chart of accounts lists every ledger account by code, grouped as assets, liabilities, equity, income and expenses. *Account roles* say which account each posting rule uses, such as Premium receivable or Unearned premium reserve.

## What happens in the accounting
The system posts to account roles, so every role the posting rules use needs an account, or those events fail to post. A new mapping starts from a date and never rewrites journals already posted. An account that journal lines use keeps its type and side, and it can be closed only when nothing is left on it.
