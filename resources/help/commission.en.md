# Commission

## What this screen is for
Commission is what the company owes the agents who brought the business, under the scheme set for the producer or the product. Each month the statement run gathers every producer's commission, bonuses, clawbacks, tax withheld and advances into one statement.

## What happens in the accounting
Commission is accrued as customers pay, as an expense and Commission payable. Approving a statement fixes the amount, and paying it through the bank, payroll or payables clears the liability; the person who approves cannot also pay. Under the zero-commission rule a salaried producer's scheme pays no commission, so nothing is accrued.

## Next step
Prepare the month's statements, have them approved, then pay them.
