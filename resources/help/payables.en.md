# Suppliers, bills and payment runs

## What this screen is for
Suppliers are the landlords, utilities, garages and surveyors the company pays, with their payment terms, tax profile and bank account. A supplier bill records their invoice by expense line, and a payment run pays the bills that fall due.

## What happens in the accounting
An approved bill posts the expense and VAT against accounts payable, less the VAT and income tax deducted at source. Releasing a payment run clears the payable from the bank account it is paid from.

## Next step
