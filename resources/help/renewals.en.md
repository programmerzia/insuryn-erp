# Renewals

## What this screen is for
Renewals, the expiry register, lists the policies that end in the next 60, 30, 15 or 7 days, by branch and producer, so none expires unnoticed. Forty-five days before expiry the system prices the renewal on the tariff in force then, raising or resetting the no-claim bonus from the policy's claims, and sends the customer a renewal notice with reminders.

## What happens in the accounting
Nothing is posted until the customer accepts: the renewal quote becomes a proposal and a new policy linked to the old one, and only that issue writes the premium. A policy that is not renewed keeps its reason, such as price or no response, for the retention report.

## Next step
Open the renewal quote and make the proposal when the customer agrees, or record why the policy was not renewed.
