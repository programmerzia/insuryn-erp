# Reports

## What this screen is for
Reports show the results of the month: trial balance, profit and loss, balance sheet, and registers such as premium by class, outstanding claims and unearned premium. Click any figure to see the ledger lines, journals and the policies or claims that make it up.

## What happens in the accounting
Reports only read the ledger and never change it. Figures for a locked month stay as they were reported.
