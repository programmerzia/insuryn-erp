# Reinsurance

## What this screen is for
Treaties set how each class's risks are shared: first the SBC compulsory share, then a quota share or surplus treaty with its reinsurers. Every policy is ceded when it is issued, endorsed or cancelled, and the cessions list shows each movement per reinsurer. A risk above the treaty's capacity is placed facultatively from the policy's Reinsurance tab.

## What happens in the accounting
A cession posts the ceded premium to the reinsurer and the reinsurance commission back, and the reinsurers' share of claims becomes a recoverable. A reinsurer statement adds up the quarter: premium ceded, less commission and claims recoverable, and the balance due to or from the reinsurer.
