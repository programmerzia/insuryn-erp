# Budgets

## What this screen is for
A budget sets the income and expense expected per account, branch and month of the fiscal year. Amounts are typed in, pasted from a spreadsheet or copied from last year's actuals, and each change after approval is a new version.

## What happens in the accounting
Nothing is posted: a budget is a plan. The variance report compares the posted actuals with the approved version for the month and the year to date.

## Next step
Send the draft for approval; someone other than the preparer approves it. Then follow the budget variance report each month.
