# Quotes, proposals and cover notes

## What this screen is for
A quote prices a risk before anything is sold: pick the product, fill in the risk details it asks for, and the premium is calculated from the tariff as you type, with every step explained. Issuing the quote gives it a number and holds the price for its validity; when the customer accepts, it becomes a proposal with the customer's identity documents.

## What happens in the accounting
Nothing is posted for a quote, a proposal or a cover note. Underwriting approves the proposal automatically unless it needs a referral, such as a sum insured above your limit or the same vehicle already insured, and then someone else decides it. A cover note is temporary evidence of cover with its own number, and the accounting starts only when the policy is issued from the proposal.

## Next step
