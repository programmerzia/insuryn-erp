# Policies

## What this screen is for
A policy is the promise to cover a customer's risk, such as a car, a factory or a cargo, for a period. You pick a product, enter the customer and the premium, and click *Issue* to give the policy its number.

## What happens in the accounting
When you issue a policy, the system records that the customer owes the premium (Premium receivable), that the cover is not yet provided (Unearned premium), and the VAT due to the government. Each month the close moves one twelfth of an annual policy's premium from unearned to income, because time has passed. You never write these entries yourself; click *View accounting* on a policy to see them.

## Next step
Take the customer's payment in *Receipts* and allocate it to the policy's installment.
