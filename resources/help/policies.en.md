# Policies

## What this screen is for
A policy is the promise to cover a customer's risk, such as a car, a factory or a cargo, for a period. Rated products start in *Quotes*: the policy is issued from the approved proposal with the premium the tariff calculated, frozen for the life of the policy.

## What happens in the accounting
When the policy is issued, the system records that the customer owes the premium (Premium receivable), that the cover is not yet provided (Unearned premium), and the VAT and stamp duty due to the government. Each month the close moves one twelfth of an annual policy's premium from unearned to income, because time has passed. A change to the risk is an endorsement that re-rates the policy and posts only the difference; open *View accounting* to see every entry.

## Next step
Take the customer's payment in *Receipts* and allocate it to the policy's installment.
