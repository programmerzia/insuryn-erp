# Refunds

## What this screen is for
When a cancelled policy leaves the customer owed money, the refund is requested here with the amount and the reason. Someone else in finance pays it from a bank account or rejects it with a reason.

## What happens in the accounting
The cancellation already moved the money owed to Customer refunds payable, so requesting a refund posts nothing. Paying it debits Customer refunds payable and credits the bank; the person who requests cannot pay the same refund.

## Next step
Once the refund is paid, match it on the bank statement in *Bank accounts*.
