# Approval and underwriting limits

## What this screen is for
*Approval limits* say who approves claim payments, their release, manual journals, reversals and other approvals above which amount, step by step. *Underwriting limits* give the largest sum insured each role may accept for each insurance class; a proposal above the limit goes to someone whose limit covers it.

## What happens in the accounting
Limits post nothing. They decide how many people must agree before money moves or a risk is accepted, and the requester can never approve their own request. A limit in force is never rewritten: a change ends it on the day the new one starts.
