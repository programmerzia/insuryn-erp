# Receipts and suspense

## What this screen is for
A receipt records money a customer paid by bank transfer, cheque, cash or mobile money, and gives it a receipt number. Allocate it to the installments it pays; money that arrives without a clear reference waits in *Suspense* until someone matches it to a policy.

## What happens in the accounting
An allocated receipt increases the bank and reduces what the customer owes (Premium receivable). Money in suspense is a liability, because it is not yet known whose money it is. If a producer on a commission scheme sold the policy, commission is accrued on the allocated amount.
