# Cheque register

## What this screen is for
Every cheque received as premium is listed here with its number, drawee bank, cheque date, receipt and state. Use it to follow cheques until the bank confirms them, and to see which ones bounced and why.

## What happens in the accounting
A cheque is recorded as a receipt and allocated to the installments it pays, like any other money received. When a cheque bounces, marking it on its receipt reverses the allocation, so the installment is owed again and any commission on it is clawed back. The bank statement is where a cheque is finally confirmed.
