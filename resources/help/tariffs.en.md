# Tariffs

## What this screen is for
A tariff is the rates and rules a quote is priced on for one insurance class: rate tables, calculation steps and duties such as VAT and stamp duty. A new tariff version starts as a draft, someone else approves it, and it is activated from a date.

## What happens in the accounting
Nothing is posted when a tariff changes. A policy keeps the premium it was issued on, frozen with the tariff version, and endorsements re-rate on that version or on the tariff in force, as the product says. The difference view shows exactly what a new version changes before anyone approves it.
